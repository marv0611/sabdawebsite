Intro

The immersive room, without the room.

SABDA Mobile takes our format out of the studio. High-fidelity immersive headphones, live musicians and projected visuals turn any space into a fully enveloping environment: an office floor, a conference hall, a hotel terrace, a brand launch.

Nothing permanent is installed. We arrive, we build, we run the session, we take it all away.

The Formats

Three immersive formats for corporate events.

01
Immersive Breathwork + Live DJ

A breathwork and movement facilitator guides the practice alongside a live musician shaping the soundscape in real time. Immersive headphones pair high-fidelity sound with light for a fully enveloping experience.

Live DJ & musician · Immersive headphones · Projected visuals · Breathwork

02
Immersive Yoga + Sound Healing

A sound healing and movement facilitator leads the practice, closing with a restorative sound bath. Immersive headphones deliver studio-grade sound, so the experience travels to any space.

Immersive headphones · Restorative yoga · Live sound bath

03
Immersive Yoga + Live DJ

Movement guided by a facilitator, scored live. A DJ builds the soundscape through the session and closes it with a restorative wind-down.

Immersive headphones · Live DJ · Restorative yoga

Common questions.

Four to six weeks is comfortable for a standard session. We have turned events around in under two weeks when the space is straightforward, so ask even if your date is close.

We recommend groups of 20 and up, and we scale to several hundred. Headcount drives the headphone count and the audio setup, so it is the first thing we confirm on the call. Above roughly 100 people we look at how the group moves through the session, and we build the technical setup around that.

A facilitator, a live musician or DJ, immersive headphones for every participant, projected visuals where the space allows, mats where the practice needs them, full setup and breakdown by our team, transport of crew and equipment, and a technical site visit before the event. The immersive setup is never quoted separately: it is the experience, not an extra.

Catering, whether that is lunch or a coffee break. Photography and video. Any additional facilitator if you want more than one discipline running. Travel beyond the Barcelona area.

An open area large enough for the whole group to sit or lie down, with power, and lift or stair access for the kit. We need a window before and after the session for setup and breakdown. Projected visuals need a wall or screen and controllable light; without that we run on sound alone, which works well.

Barcelona and the surrounding area as standard. We go further for larger events, with travel and logistics quoted separately.

Per event, not per head. The price is built from headcount, format, travel and how much production the space needs, and a minimum applies. You get a fixed proposal after the technical site visit, with no per-head surprises. Because crew, transport, coordination and the site visit are fixed costs, the cost per person drops as the group grows.
