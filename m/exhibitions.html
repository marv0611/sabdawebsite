Immersive Art Exhibitions in Barcelona

SABDA is a 360-degree projection room with Dolby Atmos spatial audio in Barcelona's Eixample. By day it hosts immersive wellness classes. The same room doubles as one of the city's most unusual exhibition spaces, and it has hosted digital art exhibitions, immersive showcases and brand experiences since 2023.

The space

Floor-to-ceiling LCD laser projections wrap the entire room. Invisible in-wall speakers and ceiling subwoofers deliver cinema-grade spatial audio. Capacity: 100 standing, 80 seated. There is no stage and no screen edge: the room itself is the medium. Artists working with us design for every surface at once, and visitors stand inside the work rather than in front of it.
