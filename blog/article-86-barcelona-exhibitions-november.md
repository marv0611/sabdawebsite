If you are visiting in late autumn, these are the Barcelona exhibitions in November 2026 worth planning a day around. November is when the city's museums open their winter season: Picasso and architecture at the Picasso Museum, World Press Photo at the CCCB, two new shows at the MNAC, and two festivals of digital and video art, while the big October openings are still running. Below: what opens, what closes, what is free, and how long to allow for each.

Every date and price was checked on each venue's official website on 28 September 2026, based on what had been announced by then. We will keep updating it as new dates are confirmed. One venue on the list is ours, and we say so where it appears.

## New in November

### Aurèlia Muñoz at MACBA, from 5 November

MACBA, the contemporary art museum in the Raval, opens "Beings", an exhibition devoted to the Barcelona textile artist Aurèlia Muñoz, on 5 November. The collection display "Spiral of Time" continues until 17 January 2027. Tickets cost €15 and cover the whole museum; entry is free from Thursday to Saturday from 18:00 and on the first Sunday of the month. Closed on Tuesdays. [macba.cat](https://www.macba.cat/es/visita)

**How long to allow:** 1.5 to 2 hours for the whole museum.

### World Press Photo at the CCCB, 6 November to 13 December

The annual World Press Photo exhibition returns to the CCCB from 6 November to 13 December 2026. Tickets cost €8 (€6 reduced), and the CCCB is free on Sundays from 15:00 to 20:00 with an online booking.

**How long to allow:** about an hour. **How to book:** online, especially for free Sunday slots.

### Two new shows at the MNAC

The Museu Nacional d'Art de Catalunya on Montjuïc opens "El viaje inverso. Un fresco sonoro en el románico" on 12 November (until 30 April 2027), and "Jujol, de Perejaume" on 26 November (until 29 March 2027). A general ticket costs €12 and is valid for two days within a month. From October, the museum opens Tuesday to Saturday 10:00 to 18:00 and Sundays 10:00 to 15:00; it is free on Saturdays from 15:00 and the first Sunday of the month, with online booking. [museunacional.cat](https://www.museunacional.cat/es/exhibitions-future)

**How long to allow:** half a day if you include the Romanesque collection.

### "Picasso and Architecture" at the Picasso Museum, from 20 November

The Museu Picasso in the Born opens "Picasso and Architecture" on 20 November 2026, running until 7 March 2027. Tickets cost €12 online. Entry is free on Thursdays from 16:00 to 19:00 and on the first Sunday of the month, but you must book; free tickets are released four days ahead at 10:00. [museupicassobcn.cat](https://museupicassobcn.cat/es/planifica-la-visita/horarios-y-precios)

**How long to allow:** about 1.5 hours with the collection. **How to book:** online, always.

### Robert Llimós at Can Framis, from 10 November

The Fundació Vila Casas shows Robert Llimós at its Can Framis museum in Poblenou from 10 November 2026 to 7 February 2027. General admission is €10, free for under-12s.

## Immersive and digital exhibitions

### SABDA's new immersive show (Eixample), open since 17 October

Full disclosure: SABDA is our venue. SABDA's new immersive show is a 25 minute journey through four worlds of life on Earth, made in Unreal Engine and projected across the four walls of a 360° room with Dolby Atmos sound, for 25 people per session at C/ Muntaner 83B in the Eixample.

The show opened on 17 October 2026 and runs through November on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays. Tickets cost €20 for adults and €12 for children, and include a welcome glass of cava or juice in SABDA Café. You travel at animal eye level and, for 25 minutes, become one of them. There is no headset. The nearest metro is Universitat. Details and tickets are on [SABDA's exhibitions page](/exhibitions/).

**How long to allow:** 25 minutes, plus the welcome drink. **How to book:** online in advance, since each session holds 25 people.

### MIRA and LOOP: November's festivals

**MIRA Digital Arts Festival, 6 and 7 November.** Audiovisual live shows, installations and 360° fulldome pieces at Fira Montjuïc, with Max Cooper, Arca, Helena Hauff, Nicola Cruz and Tortoise. A full pass costs €110 and a day pass €60, plus fees. [mirafestival.com](https://mirafestival.com/)

**LOOP Barcelona, 10 to 21 November.** The video art festival runs across museums, galleries and cinemas. The LOOP Fair, with each project shown in a hotel room, runs from 17 to 19 November. [loop-barcelona.com](https://loop-barcelona.com/)

- **Dalí Boulevard Cibernètic (Casa Amatller).** From €15 on weekdays. Allow 75 minutes.
- **White Rabbit (Passeig de Gràcia 55).** Ten immersive rooms by local artists, from €16. Allow 60 to 90 minutes.
- **IKONO (Arenas).** Photo-friendly immersive rooms, daily 12:00 to 19:00. Allow 45 to 60 minutes.
- **Museum of Banksy (Carrer Trafalgar 34).** €14, free for under-6s.

## Art museums

**La Pedrera.** "The Secret Life of Plants. Anselm Kiefer in the Grothe Collection" runs until 24 January 2027. €16 (€12 reduced), free for under-12s. Allow an hour.

**MNAC.** Besides the two new shows, the "Wounded Militiaman" exhibition and "Sim. Acquisition, Drawing and War" run until 31 December.

**CCCB.** "The Cult of Beauty" closes on 8 November, so the first week of the month is your last chance. €8 online.

**CaixaForum.** "Una fuerza del pasado", from the "la Caixa" Foundation's contemporary art collection, until 10 January 2027. €6, free for under-16s.

**Museu Tàpies.** Dora García's project runs until 29 November. €15.

**Design Museum (Disseny Hub).** "André Ricard. Design in use" from 29 October, and the Enjoia't jewellery awards until 29 November. €6.20.

**Sant Pau.** The free Gaudí centenary exhibition at the Recinte Modernista closes on 15 November.

**CosmoCaixa.** The shark exhibition "Tiburones. Los guardianes del océano", open since 22 October, is included in the €8 ticket and free for under-16s. Good for families; see our guide to [Barcelona shows for kids](/blog/barcelona-shows-for-kids/).

## Free exhibitions in November

**First Sunday: 1 November.** It is also All Saints' Day, a public holiday. The MNAC, MACBA, Picasso Museum and the city museums are free on the first Sunday; check each museum's holiday hours before you go.

**Every Sunday from 15:00.** City museums such as the MUHBA, the Museu Blau and the Maritime Museum, and the CCCB from 15:00 to 20:00.

**Always free.** La Virreina Centre de la Imatge (from 31 October, Marion Scemama and David Wojnarowicz), Palau Robert, La Fabra, Arts Santa Mònica and the Fundació Suñol.

## A November weekend in three plans

**Saturday morning on Montjuïc.** The MNAC and its two new shows, or go after 15:00 when entry is free. CaixaForum is a short walk downhill.

**Saturday afternoon in the Eixample.** Anselm Kiefer at La Pedrera, then SABDA's 25 minute immersive show a short walk away, with the welcome drink in SABDA Café to finish.

**Sunday in the Raval and Born.** World Press Photo at the CCCB, free from 15:00 with a booking, and from 20 November, "Picasso and Architecture" at the Picasso Museum.

## FAQ

**What exhibitions are on in Barcelona in November 2026?**
The main openings are "Picasso and Architecture" at the Picasso Museum (from 20 November), World Press Photo at the CCCB (from 6 November), Aurèlia Muñoz at MACBA (from 5 November) and two new shows at the MNAC. Anselm Kiefer at La Pedrera and SABDA's new immersive show continue.

**What immersive exhibitions are open in Barcelona in November?**
Casa Batlló, Moco Museum, Codi Gaudí, Iniesta Experience, Dalí Boulevard Cibernètic, IKONO, White Rabbit, Antarctica Experience and SABDA's new immersive show, plus the MIRA festival on 6 and 7 November.

**When is World Press Photo 2026 in Barcelona?**
From 6 November to 13 December 2026 at the CCCB. Tickets cost €8 (€6 reduced), and entry is free on Sundays from 15:00 to 20:00 with a booking.

**Which Barcelona museums are free in November?**
On Sunday 1 November, the first Sunday of the month and a public holiday, the MNAC, MACBA, Picasso Museum and city museums are free. City museums are also free every Sunday from 15:00, and La Virreina, Palau Robert and La Fabra are always free.

**How long is SABDA's new immersive show, and what does it cost?**
It lasts 25 minutes and costs €20 for adults and €12 for children, including a welcome glass of cava or juice in SABDA Café. It runs on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays.

**Is the Pompeii exhibition still on in Barcelona?**
No. "The Last Days of Pompeii" closed at IDEAL Montjuïc on 21 September 2026, and no replacement has been announced.

**What can I do in Barcelona in November if it rains?**
Every exhibition in this guide is indoors. A good rainy day pairs a big museum in the morning, such as the MNAC or CosmoCaixa, with a short immersive show in the afternoon.
