## What happened to teamLab in Barcelona

teamLab, the Japanese art collective behind teamLab Borderless and teamLab Planets, has shown in Barcelona twice. CaixaForum Barcelona hosted "teamLab. Art, Technology and Nature" from 17 May 2021 to 9 January 2022, with interactive works that responded to visitors' movement. [teamlab.art](https://www.teamlab.art/e/caixaforum_barcelona/)

In October 2021, Moco Museum opened its Barcelona branch with a digital art programme that included teamLab's "Flowers and People – A Whole Year per Hour", a work where a year of seasonal flowers blooms and scatters over an hour and reacts to people standing in front of it. teamLab's own website still lists it at Moco Barcelona with no end date. [teamlab.art](https://www.teamlab.art/e/mocomuseumbarcelona/)

The CaixaForum show closed in January 2022 and has not returned.

## The closest things to teamLab in Barcelona right now

What people love about teamLab is a specific mix: digital nature, room-scale imagery and interaction. No Barcelona venue does all three, but each of these delivers part of it.

### 1. Moco Museum: the teamLab connection

Moco's digital and immersive rooms in the Born are the only place in Barcelona where a teamLab work is still listed, alongside Studio Irma's mirrored infinity room "Diamond Matrix" and works by Banksy and KAWS. Moco notes that not every artwork is always on display, so if the teamLab piece is the reason you are going, ask at the desk. Tickets from €14.95, children up to 6 free. [mocomuseum.com](https://www.mocomuseum.com/barcelona/digital-immersive-art-museum-barcelona/)

### 2. SABDA's new immersive show: digital nature at room scale

Full disclosure: SABDA is our venue. SABDA's new immersive show is a 25 minute journey through four worlds of life on Earth, made in Unreal Engine and projected across the four walls of a 360° room with Dolby Atmos sound, for 25 people per session at C/ Muntaner 83B in the Eixample.

Of everything in the city, it is the closest to teamLab's digital nature: you travel at animal eye level and, for 25 minutes, become one of them, with no headset. It opened on 17 October 2026 and runs on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays, at €20 for adults and €12 for children, including a welcome glass of cava or juice in SABDA Café. It is not interactive in the teamLab sense. Details on [our exhibitions page](/exhibitions/).

### 3. Casa Batlló's Gaudí Cube: digital art in a Gaudí house

Every Casa Batlló ticket ends in the Gaudí Cube, a six-sided LED room where Refik Anadol presents "Gaudí Dreams". Different artist, similar wow. From €29, children up to 12 free. [casabatllo.es](https://www.casabatllo.es/en/experience/)

### 4. Codi Gaudí: the interactive part

For interaction, Codi Gaudí at Espai Imagina uses VR headsets and mixed reality to let you walk through buildings Gaudí never built. Until 8 January 2027, about 70 minutes, closed on Tuesdays.

### What about IDEAL?

IDEAL, the city's large projection venue, has nothing open this autumn: its Pompeii show closed on 21 September 2026 and no new exhibition has been announced.

## FAQ

**Is there a teamLab in Barcelona?**
No. There is no teamLab museum in Barcelona, and teamLab has not announced any exhibition in Barcelona or elsewhere in Spain as of October 2026.

**Was teamLab ever in Barcelona?**
Yes. CaixaForum Barcelona hosted "teamLab. Art, Technology and Nature" from 17 May 2021 to 9 January 2022, and Moco Museum has shown teamLab's "Flowers and People – A Whole Year per Hour" since October 2021.

**Is there teamLab Borderless or teamLab Planets in Barcelona?**
No. teamLab Borderless and teamLab Planets are permanent museums in other cities, and neither has a Barcelona venue.

**What is the closest thing to teamLab in Barcelona?**
Moco Museum for an actual teamLab work, SABDA's new immersive show for digital nature in a 360° room, Casa Batlló's Gaudí Cube for large-scale digital art, and Codi Gaudí for interactive VR.

**How much is SABDA's new immersive show?**
€20 for adults and €12 for children, including a welcome drink. It lasts 25 minutes and runs on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays.
