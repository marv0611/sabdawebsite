Short answer: no, Barcelona does not have a permanent teamLab museum. But the full answer is more interesting, because teamLab has exhibited here twice, pieces of that spirit are still in the city, and a few venues get surprisingly close to what makes teamLab special. Here is the complete picture.

## What happened to teamLab in Barcelona

teamLab, the Japanese art collective behind the immersive museums in Tokyo and the touring installations that fill every feed, came to Barcelona in 2021. CaixaForum hosted the exhibition teamLab: Art, Technology and Nature from May 2021 to January 2022, with two interactive works where visuals responded to visitors' movement. Around the same time, Moco Museum opened with teamLab pieces in its digital art rooms.

The CaixaForum show ended in January 2022 and never returned. Since then, "teamlab barcelona" has been a search with no good answer. Until now.

## The 4 closest things to teamLab in Barcelona right now

What people love about teamLab is a specific combination: digital nature, room-scale projection, and interaction, the art responds to you. No single Barcelona venue replicates all three, but each of these delivers part of it.

### 1. Moco Museum: the teamLab connection that stayed

Moco's digital immersive rooms in El Born have shown teamLab works, including pieces from the Moco Masters digital program, alongside Warhol, Banksy and Hirst. The rotation changes, so check what is currently on display, but this is the venue with an actual teamLab history in the city. Entry from about 16 euros. [mocomuseum.com](https://www.mocomuseum.com/tickets/barcelona/)

### 2. IDEAL: the room-scale spectacle

If what you want is the scale, a vast hall where projections cover every surface, IDEAL in Poblenou is Barcelona's biggest. It is spectator-style rather than interactive, and the current Tutankhamun exhibition (since March 2026) shows the format at full power. From 12.50 euros on weekdays. [idealbarcelona.com](https://idealbarcelona.com/en/)

### 3. SABDA: the participation

Full disclosure: this is our studio. What no other Barcelona venue offers is the third teamLab ingredient, being part of the work instead of watching it. SABDA is a 360-degree projection room with Dolby Atmos spatial audio in Eixample where you take yoga, sound healing and breathwork classes inside custom-designed visuals. You are not looking at the room, you are in it, moving through it, for a full hour. Classes from 18 euros. And from October 2026, the same room opens a new immersive art exhibition program. Details first at [our exhibitions page](/exhibitions/).

### 4. Eonarium: the atmosphere

Light shows projected inside a historic church, quieter and more contemplative than the big venues. Not interactive, but the closest thing in the city to teamLab's meditative side. [eonariumexperiences.com](https://eonariumexperiences.com/barcelona/en/)

## What about the Van Gogh immersive experience in Barcelona?

Same story, different artist. Barcelona has hosted several Van Gogh immersive shows over the years, Meet Vincent Van Gogh, El Mundo de Van Gogh, and the touring Van Gogh: The Immersive Experience. They were all temporary, and they rotate in and out of the city. As of mid-2026 there is no permanent Van Gogh immersive show running in Barcelona: IDEAL, which hosted art-focused immersive shows in the past, is currently running Tutankhamun instead.

If a Van Gogh show is the reason you searched, check IDEAL's current program first, then consider whether one of the venues above scratches the same itch. The format is identical, only the paintings change.

## The honest bottom line

Barcelona does not have a teamLab, but it has something no teamLab city has: a scene where you can watch immersive art at scale (IDEAL), see actual teamLab pieces in a museum (Moco), and then physically practice inside the projections (SABDA), all within 20 minutes of each other.
