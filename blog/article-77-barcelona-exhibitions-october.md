## New in October

### SABDA's new immersive show (Eixample), from 17 October

Full disclosure: SABDA is our venue. SABDA's new immersive show is a 25 minute journey through four worlds of life on Earth, made in Unreal Engine and projected across the four walls of a 360° room with Dolby Atmos sound, for 25 people per session at C/ Muntaner 83B in the Eixample.

The show opens on 17 October 2026 and runs on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays. Tickets cost €20 for adults and €12 for children, and include a welcome glass of cava or juice in SABDA Café. You travel at animal eye level and, for 25 minutes, become one of them. There is no headset. The nearest metro is Universitat. Details and tickets are on [SABDA's exhibitions page](/exhibitions/).

**How long to allow:** the show itself is 25 minutes; add time for the welcome drink in SABDA Café. **How to book:** online in advance, since each session holds 25 people. **Why go:** it is made in Unreal Engine, a game engine, and the short format fits into an afternoon without eating the whole day.

### Anselm Kiefer at La Pedrera, from 2 October

"The Secret Life of Plants. Anselm Kiefer in the Grothe Collection" runs at La Pedrera from 2 October 2026 to 24 January 2027, as part of the Gaudí Year programme. The exhibition ticket is €16 (€12 reduced) and free for under-12s. Open 10:00 to 19:30. [lapedrera.com](https://www.lapedrera.com/en/exhibitions/)

**How long to allow:** around an hour. **Why go:** large-scale Kiefer works in lead, ash and plant matter, inside a Gaudí building.

### Dana Lixenberg at KBr, from 1 October

The KBr photography centre in Barceloneta opens "Dana Lixenberg. American Images" alongside KBr Flama'26, a show of emerging photographers. Both run from 1 October 2026 to 17 January 2027 on one €5 ticket (€3 reduced). From October, KBr opens Tuesday to Sunday 11:00 to 19:00 and closes on Mondays. [kbr.fundacionmapfre.org](https://kbr.fundacionmapfre.org/en/)

**How long to allow:** 60 to 90 minutes for both shows. **Why go:** one of the most respected portrait photographers working today, for the price of a coffee and a pastry.

### Iniesta Experience: The Inner Game, from 28 September

The immersive experience about Andrés Iniesta opened on 28 September in the former IMAX building on Moll d'Espanya, Port Vell, and runs until 10 January 2027. Tickets from €18. [iniestaexperience.com](https://iniestaexperience.com/en/)

**How long to allow:** about 90 minutes. **Why go:** the newest immersive opening in the city, and an easy walk from the Gothic Quarter.

### Codi Gaudí at Espai Imagina, open since 22 September

"Codi Gaudí, l'Experiència Inèdita" is an extended reality experience at Espai Imagina, Av. Diagonal 177, running from 22 September 2026 to 8 January 2027. Prices vary by day, with Mondays the cheapest, and under-3s go free. [espai-imagina.com](https://www.espai-imagina.com/)

**How long to allow:** about 70 minutes.

## Immersive and digital exhibitions

**Casa Batlló, Passeig de Gràcia 43.** The general visit includes the Gaudí Cube, with Refik Anadol's "Gaudí Dreams". On the second floor, Casa Batlló Contemporary shows "Gaudí-Miró-Gomis: Deconstructed" until 10 January 2027. General visit from €29, children 0 to 12 free. **Allow:** 1 hour 15 minutes, or 1 hour 45 with the exhibition. **Book:** timed tickets online. [casabatllo.es](https://www.casabatllo.es/en/)

**Moco Museum, Carrer de Montcada 25, El Born.** Banksy, modern masters and dedicated digital and immersive art rooms. From €14.95, under-6s free. **Allow:** about an hour. **Pair it with:** the Picasso Museum next door. [mocomuseum.com](https://mocomuseum.com/)

**Dalí Boulevard Cibernètic, Casa Amatller, Passeig de Gràcia 41.** An immersive experience built around Dalí, two doors from Casa Batlló. From €15 on weekdays, €18 at weekends. **Allow:** about 75 minutes.

**IKONO, Arenas shopping centre, Gran Via 373.** Playful sensory rooms designed for photos. Open daily 12:00 to 19:00, tickets on Fever. **Allow:** about an hour.

**White Rabbit, The Off Museum, Passeig de Gràcia 55.** Ten immersive rooms on Barcelona and Catalan traditions. From €14.50. **Allow:** about an hour.

**Museum of Banksy, Carrer Trafalgar 34.** Large-scale reproductions of Banksy's work. €14, under-6s free, open daily 10:00 to 20:00. **Allow:** 45 to 60 minutes.

**Antarctica Experience, Arenas, level 4.** An immersive experience about Antarctica at GIGA Exhibitions. The official site does not list a price or closing date, so check before you go.

**Balloon Story, Espacio Inmersa, C/ Llull 119, Poblenou.** Giant inflatable rooms aimed at families, from €13.90. Closes on 4 October, so only the first weekend of the month.

## Art museums

**MNAC, Montjuïc.** "Recovered from the Enemy", on works deposited at the museum under Franco, closes on 4 October. The shows on the "Wounded Militiaman" painting and "Sim. Acquisition, Drawing and War" run until 31 December. €12 covers the collection, temporary shows and the rooftop viewpoint. Free every Saturday from 15:00 and on the first Sunday of the month. **Allow:** half a day if you want the Romanesque collection too. [museunacional.cat](https://www.museunacional.cat/en)

**MACBA, Raval.** The collection display "Spiral of Time" runs until 17 January 2027, alongside Anna Moreno, Basel Abbas and Ruanne Abou-Rahme, and Aurèlia Muñoz's kites. €15 (€13.50 online), valid for a month. Free Thursday to Saturday from 18:00 and on the first Sunday. **Allow:** 90 minutes. [macba.cat](https://www.macba.cat/en/)

**CCCB, Raval.** "The Cult of Beauty" runs until 8 November, €8 online or €10 at the desk. The European Prize for Urban Public Space is on until 15 October. Free on Sundays 15:00 to 20:00 with online booking. **Allow:** about 90 minutes. [cccb.org](https://www.cccb.org/en)

**CaixaForum, Montjuïc.** "A Force from the Past", drawn from the "la Caixa" contemporary art collection, runs from 23 September 2026 to 10 January 2027. €6, under-16s free.

**Museu Tàpies, Eixample.** "Àngel Jové. De intactu" closes on 11 October. Dora García's project runs until 29 November. General entry €15.

**Picasso Museum, El Born.** No temporary exhibition in October. "Picasso and Architecture" opens on 20 November. The collection costs €12 online and is free on Thursdays 16:00 to 19:00 and on the first Sunday, with advance booking. **Allow:** 90 minutes. **Book:** always online; the queue at the door is the longest in the city.

**Design Museum, Glòries.** The ADG Laus 2026 design awards are on show from 1 October to 1 November, with the Enjoia't jewellery awards until 29 November. "André Ricard. Design in Use" opens on 29 October. €6.20, free on Sundays 15:00 to 20:00.

**Sant Pau Recinte Modernista.** For the Gaudí centenary, "The Sagrada Família and the Hospital de Sant Pau. A Living Memory" is free at the site entrance until 15 November. From 1 October to 1 November the site also hosts Javier Pérez's installation "Folds of Time".

## FAQ

**What exhibitions are on in Barcelona in October 2026?**
The main ones are Anselm Kiefer at La Pedrera (from 2 October), Dana Lixenberg at KBr (from 1 October), "The Cult of Beauty" at the CCCB, the MACBA collection, and SABDA's new immersive show, which opens on 17 October in the Eixample.

**What immersive exhibitions are open in Barcelona now?**
In October: Casa Batlló, Moco Museum, Dalí Boulevard Cibernètic, IKONO, White Rabbit, the Museum of Banksy, Codi Gaudí, Iniesta Experience and, from 17 October, SABDA's new immersive show. Balloon Story closes on 4 October.

**Is the Pompeii exhibition still on in Barcelona?**
No. "The Last Days of Pompeii" closed at IDEAL Montjuïc on 21 September 2026, and no replacement has been announced.

**How long does SABDA's new immersive show last, and what does it cost?**
The show lasts 25 minutes and costs €20 for adults and €12 for children, including a welcome glass of cava or juice in SABDA Café. It runs on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays from 17 October 2026.

**Which Barcelona exhibitions are free?**
La Virreina, Palau Robert and La Fabra are always free. On Sunday 4 October, the first Sunday of the month, the MNAC, MACBA, Picasso Museum and several city museums are free, some with advance booking.

**Do I need to book exhibitions in advance in Barcelona?**
For Casa Batlló, the Picasso Museum and anything with small sessions, yes. SABDA's new immersive show holds 25 people per session, so booking ahead is the safe option. Most other museums have tickets at the door.

**What can I do in Barcelona in October if it rains?**
Every exhibition in this guide is indoors. A good rainy day pairs one big museum, such as the MNAC or CCCB, with a shorter immersive show in the afternoon.
